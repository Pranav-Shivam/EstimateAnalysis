# B2B Quote Turnaround Time and Win Rate

Researched 2026-09-27, backs the "Slow" problem statement and the business-case numbers in `README.md`.

## Speed-to-quote win rate

**Baytech Consulting, "From Days to Minutes: Custom CPQ and Sales Velocity"**
https://www.baytechconsulting.com/blog/from-days-to-minutes-custom-cpq-sales-velocity.md

- 78% of B2B buyers award their business to the first vendor to respond with a credible proposal.
- Average B2B manufacturer response time: 24-72 hours.
- Sales orgs responding within 5 minutes are 21x more likely to qualify the prospect vs. waiting 30 minutes.
- Responses past 24 hours: roughly 60x lower qualification rate vs. 1-hour response.
- Buyers rarely tell a vendor they lost on speed alone; they just say they chose someone else.

**Proton.ai, "RFQ Automation Software"** (distributor interviews)
https://www.proton.ai/blog/rfq-automation-software

- Median written-quote return time: 13-14 hours. Average closer to 30 hours.
- Sales leader quote: "whoever quotes first probably wins, even at a slightly higher price."
- Manual RFQ processing ~15 minutes per quote, drops to ~45 seconds with automation.
- Large multi-line bids: two weeks manually, same-day review with automation.

**Distribution Strategy Group, "Beyond the Spreadsheet"**
https://distributionstrategy.com/beyond-the-spreadsheet-transforming-order-quote-processes/

- Distributors lose 40%+ of potential wins once a quote takes longer than 24 hours.
- 50% of buyers choose whoever responds first, regardless of minor price differences.
- Sourcing staff spend ~75% of their time on data preparation, not strategic analysis.
- One case: complex multi-line quotes took up to 8 hours manually.

## Speed protects price, not just win rate

**Broadn, "Fast Quotes Boost Manufacturing Win Rates by Half"**
https://www.broadn.io/blogs/fast-quoting-increases-manufacturing-win-rates

- Manufacturers who quote fast can price up to 3% higher without losing the customer.

## Additional stats found via search (not independently fetched, treat as secondary)

- Average B2B response time cited elsewhere as ~42 hours (broader industry figure, vs. Proton.ai's 13-14hr median specific to distributors interviewed).
- Freight forwarding vertical: ~90hr average response time, winning forwarders respond in under 30 minutes; useful as a cross-industry comparison point, not directly about plumbing/HVAC distribution.
- Responding within the first minute (vs. later) can boost lead conversion by ~391%; general sales stat, not quote-specific, use cautiously.

## RFQ automation ROI (named companies)

**Go Autonomous, RFQ & Quote Automation**
https://goautonomous.io/use-cases/rfq-quote-automation/

- Mediq: 91% of commercial requests (quotes and orders) handled autonomously, no manual involvement.
- Danfoss: quotes and orders executed in under 1 minute across 26 countries after deploying autonomous commerce.
- General claim across sources: automation lets reps handle ~5x more RFQs in the same time; document-processing time cut up to 92%; typical go-live 6-12 weeks.
- Generic ROI figures (250-300% within 24 months) found across multiple vendor blogs; vendor-reported, treat as directional not verified.
